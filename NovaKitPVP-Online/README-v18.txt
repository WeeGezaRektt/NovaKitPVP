NovaKitPVP V18 — platform filters

Adds three public filters beside the search bar:
- Full Tierlist
- Bedrock Only
- Java Only

Detection:
- usernames beginning with "." are Bedrock/Floodgate players
- all other usernames are Java players

The filter works on Overall and individual kit pages.
Overall positions are renumbered after filtering.
The selected filter is remembered in the browser.
